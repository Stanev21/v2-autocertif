## FAQ Section
**Layout:** Two-column (text on left, FAQ list on right)

**Q1: What is Session Replay?**
A: "Session Replay is a video replay of an individual user's session on your website. It allows you to see how users experience your site from their point of view. This includes seeing the user's own clicks, hovers, and scrolls, and how any errors appeared on their screen."

**Q2: What is the main purpose of Session Replay?**
A: "The purpose of Session Replay is to confirm a specific hypothesis about the user experience, or to understand why users are behaving a certain way. For this reason, Session Replay is often used to identify struggles. With Impact Quantification, you can also quantify any struggle to see how many customers face the same problem."

**Q3: What are session replay tools in web analytics?**
A: "Session replay tools record and play back user interactions on a website, offering a visual representation of individual user sessions."

**Q4: How do session replay tools contribute to user experience improvement?**
A: "Session replay tools allow you to observe real user interactions and understand how visitors engage with your website."

**Q5: What types of data do session replay tools capture?**
A: "Session replay tools capture a variety of data, including mouse movements, clicks, scrolls, and form inputs."

**Q6: How can session replay tools aid in troubleshooting website issues?**
A: "Session replay tools help you troubleshoot issues by visually inspecting user sessions."

**Q7: What considerations are important when implementing session replay tools?**
A: "When implementing session replay tools, it's crucial to prioritize user privacy and data security."
