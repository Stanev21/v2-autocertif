## Allowances / Metrics Definitions

**Layout:** Accordion or expandable FAQ-style definitions.

**Monthly Sessions:**
"A session is a visitor's full journey across every page (or screen). It's captured and analyzed on heatmaps, funnels, and more. This is the amount of sessions you get per month for your entire account (and all sites)."

**Replays:**
"Replays or Session Replay allows you to watch how users interact with your site, such as where they click, hover, swipe, and see potential issues. It's a fixed amount per month -- it can't be increased."

**Monthly Responses (VoC):**
"Responses from all your surveys and feedback widgets. This is the amount you get per month for your entire account (and all sites)."

**User Tests/Interviews:**
"You can get feedback directly from your users with interviews and unmoderated tests. This is the amount you get per month for interviews, tests answered, or both. It's a fixed amount, and it can't be increased."
