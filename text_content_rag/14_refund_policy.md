# Refund Policy - /refund-policy

Effective as of January 2025

## Title
"Refund Policy"
"Clear and fair guidelines for refunds"

---

## Purpose

"The refund policy of autocertif.com, operated by LADEV LTD., aims to ensure customer satisfaction by providing clear and fair guidelines for the refund of products or services."

---

## Refund Eligibility

- Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of the purchase date
- A refund request only applies to the payment for the trial period and not the subscription
- Reference to Article L221-28 of the French Consumer Code regarding withdrawal rights for digital content

---

## Refund Request Process

- Customer must contact service via contact form
- Customer service provides refund request form
- Customer returns product or provides necessary information

---

## Exclusions

- Perishable or consumable products ineligible
- Customized or specially ordered products cannot be refunded except for defects

---

## Refund Timeline

"Once autocertif.com has received the returned product or the refund request has been approved, the refund will be processed within 7 business days."

---

## Refund Method

Refunds via same original payment method unless alternative requested.

---

## Order Cancellation

- Contact customer service before shipment for full refund
- Full refund if order not yet processed

---

## Policy Changes

"Autocertif.com, operated by LADEV LTD., reserves the right to modify this refund policy at any time."
