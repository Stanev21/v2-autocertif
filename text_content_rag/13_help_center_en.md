## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does Autocertif work?**
Autocertif cross-references 2,500+ checkpoints from 8,000+ sources across 25+ countries. Simply enter a VIN number or license plate, and we generate a comprehensive vehicle history report in ~55 seconds on average. The report includes mileage verification, accident history, theft records, vehicle inspection, emissions data, and more.

**What is a VIN and where can I find it?**
A VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) is a unique 17-character code assigned to every vehicle. You can find it on the driver's side dashboard (visible through the windshield), on the driver's side door jamb sticker, on the vehicle registration document, or on the insurance card. It contains no letters I, O, or Q to avoid confusion with numbers.

**How long does it take to receive my report?**
Most reports are generated in ~55 seconds on average. In rare cases where data from certain sources takes longer to compile, it may take up to a few minutes. You'll receive an email notification as soon as your report is ready.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. For business clients purchasing bulk packages, we also offer bank transfer as a payment option.

**Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?**
If your report contains incomplete or inaccurate data, you can contact our support team with your order number and a description of the issue. Refund eligibility is assessed on a case-by-case basis. Please review our Refund Policy for full details.

**What countries does Autocertif cover?**
Autocertif covers vehicles registered in over 25 countries, with particularly strong coverage across the European Union and the United Kingdom. Data availability may vary by country depending on local registries and data sources.

**How accurate is the mileage verification?**
Our mileage verification cross-references data from official vehicle inspections, service records, insurance databases, and other verified sources. We flag any discrepancies or suspicious rollbacks. While no system is 100% infallible, our multi-source approach provides one of the most reliable mileage checks available on the market.

**Do you offer solutions for businesses?**
Yes! We offer tailored solutions for car dealerships, insurance companies, leasing firms, and other automotive businesses. This includes bulk report packages, API integration for your existing systems, dedicated account management, and custom pricing. Visit our Business page or contact our sales team for more information.
