## Three critical checks

Each report begins with three essential verification areas.

**01 - Mileage & Odometer**
Easily verify a vehicle's actual mileage, backed by data from 8,000+ trusted sources across 25+ countries.

**02 - Damage**
We check over 300 million records to show you where and when damage occurred. And what repairs may have cost.

**03 - Theft**
Cross-referencing with international stolen vehicle registries and official databases.

## Every Detail, Verified

### Mileage & Odometer
- Odometer rollback detection across multiple data points
- Mileage prediction based on historical patterns
- Driving habits analysis showing highway vs. city use
- Cross-border mileage verification for imported vehicles

### Accidents & Damage
- Over 300 million accident records worldwide
- Visual damage diagram showing impact zones
- Repair cost estimates and damage severity ratings
- Insurance claim history and total loss records

### Theft Records
- International registry cross-reference
- Real-time stolen vehicle registry checks
- Recovery status and history tracking
- Cross-border theft verification

### Vehicle Inspection
- Official inspection results and history
- Reported defects and repair requirements
- Pass/fail status for each inspection
- Next inspection due date tracking

## Complete Picture

### Fuel Consumption & CO2
- Urban, extra-urban, and combined fuel consumption
- CO2 emission class rating (A to G scale)
- CO2 emissions in g/km
- Energy label and environmental impact

### Natural Disasters
- Flood exposure verification
- Hail and storm damage checks
- Regional natural disaster cross-reference
- Historical disaster event mapping
