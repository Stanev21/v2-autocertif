## FAQ

### Title
"Frequently Asked Questions" / "Common questions about cancellation."

### Will I be charged again?
"No. Once cancelled, no further charges will be made. Your subscription stays active until the end of the current billing period."

### Can I still access my reports?
"Yes. All reports you have already generated remain accessible in your account."

### Can I resubscribe later?
"Yes, you can reactivate your subscription at any time from the pricing page."

### Am I eligible for a refund?
"Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of purchase."
