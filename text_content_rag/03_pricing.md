## FAQ

### Q: What is included in a vehicle history report?
"Every Autocertif report includes mileage verification, accident and damage history, theft records, vehicle inspection history, fuel consumption and CO2 emissions data, and natural disaster checks. We pull data from over 8,000 sources across 25+ countries."

### Q: How quickly will I receive my report?
"Reports are generated in ~55 seconds on average. Once purchased, you'll have instant access to your complete vehicle history report, which you can view online or download as a PDF."

### Q: What if no data is found for my vehicle?
"If a license plate search returns no results, we recommend using the VIN number which guarantees a complete report. The VIN provides access to all available data for the vehicle."

### Q: Is there a money-back guarantee?
"If you experience an issue with your report, please contact our support team. Refund eligibility is assessed on a case-by-case basis. Please review our Refund Policy for full details."
