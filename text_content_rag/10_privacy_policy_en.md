## Article 1 – Definitions

### 1.1 Technical terms
- **Consent**: freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous indication of the data subject's wishes
- **Data Controller**: entity determining purposes and means of processing
- **Processor**: entity processing data on behalf of controller
- **Processing**: operations performed on personal data
- **Personal data breach**: breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure

### 1.2 Other terms
- **Subscription**: subscription to LADEV LTD. services
- **Client**: contracting party subscribed to services
- **Services**: services provided by Data Controller
- **Website**: autocertif.com
- **User**: individual using Website

## Article 2 – What is personal data?

Personal data refers to any information that can directly or indirectly identify a natural person (surname, first name, email, telephone number, postal address, etc.).

GDPR defines it as: any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person; an identifiable natural person is one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, in particular by reference to an identifier such as a name, an identification number, location data, an online identifier or to one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural or social identity.

## Article 3 – Data of minors

Only minors aged 15 or over may consent to personal data processing per Article 8 of GDPR and French Data Protection Act.

Users under 15 must obtain consent from legal representative for data collection and Website service use.

Data Controller not liable if users provide data concerning minors under 15.

## Article 4 – Collection of personal data

User consent collected. When browsing, users provide personal data for indicated purposes.

By providing their data, the User therefore consents to the collection and processing of the personal data provided by the Data Controller for the exclusive purposes as detailed in Article 5.

Data Controller collects and processes strictly necessary data. Undertakes not to process for purposes other than mentioned. If offering new services requiring data collection, will collect consent again.

Takes reasonable steps to ensure accuracy and relevance of data.

## Article 5 – Purpose of processing

### Contact form
Users provide surname, first name, email. Processed for: Managing contact request; Responding to and processing request. Legal basis: User consent.

### Account creation
Users provide: surname, first name, email, telephone number, address, billing address. Processed for: Validating account creation; Providing services; Managing closures; Ensuring maintenance and assistance. Legal basis: User consent and contract performance.

### Subscription payment
Users provide: surname, first name, email, telephone number, billing address. Processed for: Validating order; Providing subscription services; Managing disputes and after-sales service. Legal basis: Contract performance.

### Analysis of browsing information
Connection data and browsing information used for statistical purposes (website traffic, user experience analysis) and cookie policy purposes. Legal basis: Legitimate interests for smooth browsing experience; compliance with legal obligations for fraud prevention.

## Article 6 – Data retention period

Data retained only as long as necessary for processing purposes. Retention periods:

- User data: maximum 3 years after last contact or earlier if rights exercised
- Client data: maximum 5 years after subscription order
- Billing data: up to 10 years per regulatory deadlines
- Connection data: maximum 13 months

Reference grid developed using CNIL recommendations. May retain data to fulfill legal obligations, enable rights exercise, or for statistical purposes. Upon expiry, data deleted or anonymised.

## Article 8 – Data protection

The Data Controller has taken all useful and necessary precautions, with regard to the current state of the art, to protect your information in a secure environment.

No method of transmission over Internet or electronic storage completely secure. Cannot guarantee absolute security. Would notify affected users of security breaches.

Incident notification procedures account for legal obligations at national or European level.

If integrity and confidentiality compromised, will comply with French Data Protection Act and GDPR procedures.

Does not resell or outsource user data.

## Article 9 – Rights of individuals

Per French Data Protection Act and GDPR, users have:

### 9.1 Right of access, rectification and erasure
May access, update, modify, or request deletion of personal data by contacting personal data processing officer per GDPR Articles 15, 16, 17, 19.

### 9.2 Right to data portability
May request portability of personal data to another website per GDPR Article 20.

### 9.3 Right to restriction and objection
May request restriction or object to processing. Controller cannot refuse unless demonstrating legitimate, compelling grounds overriding user interests per GDPR Articles 18, 21.

### 9.4 Right not to be subject to automated decision
Per Regulation 2016/679, user has right not to be subject to solely automated decisions producing legal effects or significantly affecting them.

### 9.5 Right to determine fate of data
May organise what happens to collected and processed data upon death per French Law No. 2016-1321 of 7 October 2016.

To exercise access, rectification, erasure, restriction, consent withdrawal, portability, and objection rights, send request:

- Via contact form; or
- By post to: ul. Cherkovna 57, floor 2, office 15, Sofia, Bulgaria

Include surname, first name, address, and proof of identity.

Processed within one month unless compelling, justified grounds warrant extension.

## Article 11 – Cookie policy

### 11.1 What is a Cookie?
A cookie is a small file placed on the terminal of any user (computer, tablet or mobile device) by the Website when it is visited.

Does not contain personal information but allows link between device and usage/experience preferences (location, language, font size).

### 11.2 User consent
Consent always requested for cookies involving saving and analysing personal data. Valid for 13 months unless exceptions, then new authorisation requested.

## Article 12 – Use of Cookies

Per CNIL resolution No. 2013-378, cookies record information stored in hard drive memory. Generate audience statistics and offer services based on previous selections.

Alert banner asks each visitor whether accepting cookies. To guarantee free, informed, unambiguous consent, banner won't disappear until continued browsing and configured choices.

Without prior user consent, cookies not placed or read if:
- Person doesn't continue browsing (absence of action not manifestation of intent); or
- Person clicks link to configure cookies and refuses placement.

Uses and collects cookies for:
- Processing statistics and Website traffic information, optimising it
- Offering smooth browsing experience by adapting presentation to device preferences
- Storing information provided by user
