## What We Check

Report coverage

Each report covers six key areas of vehicle history analysis.

Learn more

**Mileage**: Odometer verification using data from 8,000+ sources across 25+ countries. Includes mileage trend chart and driving pattern analysis.

**Damages**: Analysis of over 300 million records for damage history, repair records, and write-off status. Includes visual damage diagram.

**Theft**: Cross-referencing with international stolen vehicle registries and official databases.

**Vehicle Inspection**: Vehicle inspection history, test results, and any reported defects from official technical control records.

**Fuel & CO2 Emissions**: Fuel consumption data (urban, extra-urban, combined) and CO2 emission class rating for the vehicle.

**Natural Disasters**: Verification of the vehicle's exposure to natural disasters (floods, hail, storms) in its registration area.

## Data You Can Trust

Powered by 8,000+ verified data sources

Our reports draw from government registration and title records, insurance databases covering claims and total losses, and automotive auction platforms.
